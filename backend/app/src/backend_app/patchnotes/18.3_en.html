TFT Patch 18.3 Notes

Patch: 18.3 · Released: September 22, 2026

Patch 18.3 brings champion and trait balance changes, buffs to combat Augments, nerfs to several Forest Spirits, a large collection of bug fixes, and additional cosmetics.

Mid-Patch Update

September 28

A new strategy allowed players to consistently obtain 2-star 5-cost champions much earlier than intended. This strategy was enabled by bugs involving the Polymorph+ Forest Spirit.

Polymorph+ Temporarily disabled until Patch 18.4.

September 24 — Patch 18.3B

Patch 18.3B addresses recent meta developments that were missed in the original patch as well as balance changes that were accidentally reverted when moving from 18.2B to 18.3.

Balance Changes

  • Darkbramble — Base damage amplification from sacrificing AP: 14% → 12%
  • Kha'Zix — Base Ability damage: 285/400/580 → 265/370/535
  • Kha'Zix — Isolated target damage: 310/445/660 → 285/410/605
  • Thorn — Cast time slightly reduced.

Champion Targeting

The subtle champion-targeting change introduced in Patch 18.2 has been reverted. The change had caused unexpected behavior for melee champions at the beginning of combat.

Reverted 18.2B Changes Restored

  • Camille — Ability damage: 160/240/410/700 → 150/225/375/640
  • LeBlanc — Clone chance: 10/15/40% → 10/15/30%
  • Teemo — Small Mushroom damage: 60/90/135 → 55/82/130
  • Ashe — Trail duration: 4 sec → 3 sec
  • Ashe — Base damage over time: 5/8 → 9/14
  • Draven — Bounty Hunter
    • 4-cost unit casts: 5 → 6
    • 8-cast reward: 10 rerolls → 6 rerolls
    • 5-cost unit attacks: 50 → 60
    • 7-cost damage requirement: 8,000 → 10,000
    • 12-cost eliminations: 6 → 8

Temporarily Disabled Augments

  • Challenger's Grace — Disabled due to a bug.
  • Dark Ritual — Temporarily disabled.
  • Infinite Protection — Disabled due to a bug.
  • Russian Dolls — Disabled due to a bug.

Additional Augment Changes

  • Vertical traits now require at least 4 units of that trait to be offered.
  • Emblem Sharing can no longer give the same emblem to multiple players.
  • Emblem Sharing can no longer provide emblems for traits that are too small.
  • Emblem Sharing can no longer provide Colossus, Bruiser, or Defender emblems.
  • Three-Star Service no longer grants a Tiny Champion Duplicator.
  • The empowered hex from Spotlight has been renamed Championship Hex.

Bug Fixes

Gameplay

  • Rewarded Waiting can no longer give the same champion to multiple players.
  • Craftable and non-craftable Emblems can now correctly be Reforged into one another.
  • Golden Egg can now be received correctly when obtained through Chaos Call with a full bench.
  • Planned Loss can no longer function during PvE rounds.
  • Silvermere no longer activates during carousel rounds.
  • Future's Investment now grants its rewards at Stage 2-7 instead of Stage 3-1.
  • The Jungle monster selector can now be used correctly during the arrival phase with Omega Jungle Monster.
  • Tier 1 no longer grants two copies of Akali.
  • Rengar's Rivals tooltip now correctly updates with the Rivalry Forest Spirit.
  • Uncraftable items no longer have extra space underneath their tooltip.
  • Fixed multiple Pandora's Bench and Expedition interactions involving units waiting to transform.
  • Fixed Pandora's Bench incorrectly moving twice as many champions in and out of the available champion pool.
  • Double Up random component Armory icons no longer incorrectly display Rabadon's Deathcap.
  • Unit death animations no longer abruptly stop after a combat round ends.
  • Carousel units no longer occasionally disappear after the carousel round ends.
  • Forest Life Flower can no longer be moved onto the bench by a bot during the preparation phase.
  • PvE monsters should now consistently appear correctly.
  • Fixed an issue where 3-Star Ashe could become unusable after being transformed by Polymorph.
  • Champion names no longer wrap incorrectly when dragging items over them.
  • Removed extra spacing around the shop lock tooltip.
  • Fixed an issue where purchase audio could trigger when a champion gained a Star level during preparation.
  • Fixed an issue where enemy health bars could sometimes appear above allied health bars during combat.
  • Fixed incorrect Darkbramble trait information at Trait level 6.
  • Fixed an issue that could cause PvE units to be completely absent from a PvE round.

Performance & Stability

  • Giant's Belt no longer displays at low resolution when equipped.
  • Fixed an issue where collapsing the UI immediately after Augment selection could cancel the selected Augment.
  • Fixed chat window behavior when opening and interacting with the scrollbar.
  • Fixed a crash related to the native graphics API that affected approximately 1% of active TFT games.
  • Mobile: Main menu music now stops correctly after disconnecting and returning to the title screen.
  • Fixed inconsistent progression for the Primal Pulse mission.
  • Mobile: Bench units can now be moved normally while the Augment Armory is open on affected devices.
  • Added a ping latency indicator for monitoring packet transmission delays.
  • Fixed Armories occasionally overlapping the shop after reconnecting to a game.
  • Fixed units sometimes failing to sell when dragged over the XP/Reroll interface.
  • Fixed an issue that could prevent players from returning to their board after reconnecting.
  • Champion elimination animations no longer abruptly stop after combat ends.
