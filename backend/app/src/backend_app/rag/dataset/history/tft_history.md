# Teamfight Tactics History

## Origins

Teamfight Tactics (TFT) originated as an auto-battler genre that emerged from Defense of the Ancients (DotA 2) community mods. The first major auto-battler was Dota Auto Chess, created by a modder known as Drodo in 2019, which became wildly popular in the DotA 2 community.

## Official Launch by Riot Games

Riot Games developed their own auto-battler game mode called Teamfight Tactics, launching it for League of Legends players in June 2019 as a limited beta. The response was overwhelmingly positive, with millions of players enjoying the strategic, game-of-chance gameplay.

## Evolution of TFT

Since its inception, TFT has evolved through multiple "sets," each introducing new mechanics, units, traits, and gameplay systems. Each set is distinct and typically lasts about 2-3 months before the next set launches.

## Cultural Impact

TFT has become a major esports title, with Riot Games investing heavily in professional tournaments. The game has established itself alongside League of Legends and Valorant as a key esports property.

## Global Expansion

From its initial League of Legends platform integration, TFT has expanded to standalone mobile clients and is available on multiple regions worldwide, making it one of the most accessible strategy games globally.
