# TFT Esports Overview

## Professional Scene

Teamfight Tactics has developed into a major esports title with professional tournaments across multiple regions, including North America, Europe, Asia, and others.

## Tournament Structure

TFT tournaments typically feature:
- Qualification rounds where players compete for spots
- Group stages with round-robin matches
- Playoffs with single or double-elimination brackets
- Grand finals determining regional champions

## Regional Competitions

Major regions include:
- North America (NA)
- Europe (EU)
- Korea (KR)
- China (CN)
- Southeast Asia (SEA)

Each region develops its own meta and playstyle, leading to interesting international matchups.

## World Championships

Riot Games hosts annual world championships where the best players and teams from each region compete for the global title and significant prize pools.

## Streaming and Content

Professional TFT matches are broadcast on platforms like Twitch and YouTube, with top players also creating educational content and high-ranked ladder streams.

## Prize Pools

Professional TFT tournaments offer substantial prize pools, attracting top talent and creating career opportunities for skilled players.
