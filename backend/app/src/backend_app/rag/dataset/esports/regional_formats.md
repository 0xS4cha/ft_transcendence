# Regional TFT Competitions

## North America (NA)

North American TFT features:
- High competition level
- Multiple tournament series per set
- Strong streamer ecosystem
- Accessible regional tournaments

## Europe (EU)

European competitive scene includes:
- Strong individual talent
- Multiple national scenes
- Regional tournament circuits
- Distinct meta developments

## Korea (KR)

Korean TFT features:
- Extremely high mechanical skill
- Strong early-season meta definition
- PC-room culture influence
- Tournament prestige

## China (CN)

Chinese TFT includes:
- Large player base
- Regional server focus
- Unique meta developments
- Growing esports infrastructure

## Southeast Asia (SEA)

SEA competitions feature:
- Growing competitive scene
- Regional tournament series
- International exposure opportunities
- Developing professional ecosystem

## Other Regions

Additional regions organize:
- Local and national tournaments
- Development pathways
- Growing esports infrastructure
- Participation in international events

## Regional Differences

Each region develops distinct:
- Meta approaches
- Playstyle emphasis
- Tournament structures
- Economic models

## International Representation

Regional competitions feed international tournament participation, connecting local scenes to global competition.
