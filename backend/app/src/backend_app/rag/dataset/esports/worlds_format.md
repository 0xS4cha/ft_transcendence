# TFT World Championships Format

## World Championship Structure

Riot Games hosts annual TFT World Championships bringing together the best players from each region.

## Regional Qualification

Players earn championship points throughout the season:
- Points awarded based on tournament placements
- Regional competitions feed into international championship
- Top players from each region qualify

## Championship Format

Typical championship formats include:
- Group stage round-robin matches
- Playoff bracket for top players
- Grand finals determining world champion

## Set Representation

Championships typically occur mid-set to allow player adaptation but before meta becomes too entrenched.

## Prize Distribution

Championship prize pools distribute rewards among top finishers, with grand champion receiving substantial first-place prize.

## International Competition

Worlds provides opportunities for:
- Cross-regional matchups and rivalries
- Showcasing regional meta differences
- Determining true global best players

## Broadcast Coverage

World Championships receive extensive broadcast coverage on streaming platforms with professional commentary.

## Cultural Significance

Winning Worlds is the highest achievement in professional TFT, determining the world champion and providing permanent legacy.

## Regional Development

Worlds helps develop regional competitive scenes as players gain exposure through championship participation.

## Consecutive World Winners

Tracking multiple world champions shows skill longevity and consistent excellence across sets.
