# Late Game Strategy

## Final Decisions (Stage 5+)

Late-game strategy focuses on maximizing the strength of your finalized composition and executing your win condition.

## Leveling to Nine

Some compositions require reaching player level 9 for access to the most powerful units. This is an expensive commitment but can pay off with tier-1 carries.

## Hyperroll Strategy

Hyperroll involves sacrificing economy to aggressively reroll for specific units. This is high-risk but can secure crucial upgrades.

## Three-Star Units

Securing three-star units requires nine total copies. This is challenging but extremely powerful when achieved.

## Positioning Adjustments

In late-game, positioning becomes even more critical as unit stats are high and each matchup consequence is amplified.

## Itemization Finalization

Ensure your carry units have optimal items before entering playoffs. Late-game item switches should be strategic and purposeful.

## Health Management

Managing health carefully is crucial. In late-game, a single loss can eliminate you. Understand your win conditions and loss consequences.

## Playoff Navigation

As lobbies progress through playoffs (final rounds), playing the meta and understanding opponent compositions becomes increasingly important.

## Win-Out Requirements

Understanding what outcomes you need in late-game helps you make informed decisions about rolling, leveling, and positioning.
