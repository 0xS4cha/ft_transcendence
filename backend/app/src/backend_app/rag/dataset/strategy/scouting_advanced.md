# Advanced Scouting

## Information Gathering

Successful players constantly gather information by:
- Viewing opponent boards between rounds
- Analyzing what units are being purchased
- Tracking trait combinations
- Identifying item distributions

## Economic Scouting

Understanding opponents' gold counts helps predict:
- Whether they'll roll or level
- Their ability to stabilize
- Their threat level

## Composition Recognition

Experienced players quickly identify opponent composition direction and can anticipate their win conditions and counters.

## Meta Understanding

Knowing the meta helps predict what competitors will play, allowing you to position your own team accordingly.

## Player Pool Analysis

Tracking which units are being used by other players helps identify high-demand units and adapt your shopping strategy.

## Adaptation Based on Intel

Intelligence gathering should directly inform your decisions:
- Pivot to counter common comps
- Avoid comps that are heavily played
- Exploit predictable patterns

## Late-Game Positioning Strategy

Knowing exactly which opponents you'll face and their compositions allows precise positioning calculations for win conditions.

## Bluffing and Information Denial

Advanced players sometimes make plays that mislead opponents about their direction, creating tactical advantages.
