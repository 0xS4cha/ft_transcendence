# Early Game Strategy

## First Round (Stage 1)

The first round is crucial for setting the foundation of your game. Players should:
- Collect component items that appear as loot
- Plan which direction they want to pivot
- Maintain flexibility while building towards a strategy

## Reroll Strategy in Early Game

Early game rerolls should be minimal, focusing on collecting items and understanding what units are available in the shop.

## Leveling Decisions

Early game leveling is typically conservative, maintaining gold efficiency while gradually leveling to access new unit types.

## Direction Selection

By stage 2, players should have a general direction but remain open to pivoting based on available units and items.

## Win Streaking Early

Securing wins early provides income and potential positioning advantages. Some comps are naturally strong early and can be used strategically.

## Loss Streaking Strategy

Some players intentionally take losses early to bank gold, accepting a weaker position to build stronger mid-game positioning.

## Item Prioritization

Early game items should support your planned composition while being transferable if you pivot directions.

## Shop Reading

Analyzing the shop helps players determine what units and directions are viable based on what others might be building.
