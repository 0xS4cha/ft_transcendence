# Rolling Strategy

## Rolling

Rolling means spending gold to refresh the shop.

## Roll When

Consider rolling when:

- Your board is significantly weaker than the lobby.
- You need a critical upgrade.
- You have reached the level where your desired unit has favorable shop odds.
- You are trying to stabilize before losing too much player health.

## Avoid Excessive Rolling

Rolling without a clear objective can destroy your economy without producing
a meaningful board improvement.

## Stabilization

A stabilization roll should prioritize the upgrades that produce the largest
immediate increase in combat power.
