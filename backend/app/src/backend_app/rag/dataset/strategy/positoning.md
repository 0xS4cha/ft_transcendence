# Positioning

## Frontline

Durable units generally belong toward the front of the formation.

## Backline

Fragile damage dealers generally benefit from distance from enemy frontline
units.

## Assassination / Backline Access

Against compositions capable of reaching the backline, positioning may need
to protect key carries rather than simply placing them in the safest-looking
corner.

## Targeting

Positioning can influence which enemy units your units encounter and target.

## Scouting

Positioning should be adjusted after observing opponents.
