# Mid-Game Strategy

## Stage 3 and 4 Gameplay

The mid-game is where compositions take shape. Players have committed to a general direction and are refining their teams.

## Leveling Priorities

Mid-game leveling depends on composition:
- Fast levelers push to higher player levels quickly
- Standard levelers balance gold and experience
- Economy-focused players save gold for rolls later

## Composition Refinement

During mid-game, players should:
- Replace early-game placeholders with core units
- Finalize trait synergies
- Optimize item placement

## Rolling Decisions

Strategic rolling at specific power spikes helps secure needed units before transitioning to late-game economy management.

## Board Strength Evaluation

Players must assess whether their board is strong enough to win streak, loss streak, or maintain stability.

## Adaptation and Pivoting

Mid-game is the last opportunity to pivot to a new direction without significant gold waste. Reading the lobby is crucial.

## Economic Decisions

Balancing between buying power (units for immediate strength) and economy (saving for rolls and leveling) is critical in mid-game.

## Positioning Adjustments

As opponents' compositions become clearer, position your team to counter common enemy compositions in your lobby.
