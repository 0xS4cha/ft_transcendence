# Leveling Strategy

## Purpose

Leveling increases the player's team size and changes shop probabilities.

## General Principles

Level when the additional board strength is worth more than the gold and
future economy sacrificed by purchasing experience.

## Fast Leveling

Fast leveling prioritizes reaching higher player levels quickly.

Advantages:
- Earlier access to expensive units
- Larger board size
- Stronger late-game potential

Disadvantages:
- Less gold available for rolling
- Potentially weaker upgraded low-cost units

## Slow Leveling

Slow leveling preserves economy and may prioritize rolling at a particular
level.

This strategy is especially useful when a composition depends on several
low-cost units.
