# TFT Economy

## Gold

Gold is used to purchase units, reroll the shop, and purchase experience.

## Interest

Players can receive additional gold based on their saved gold.

## Economy Strategy

Maintaining a strong economy can provide flexibility later in the game.

Common economic decisions include:

- Saving gold for interest
- Spending gold to stabilize a weak board
- Spending gold to level
- Rolling for upgrades
- Preserving a win streak
- Preserving a loss streak

## Win Streaks

A player may intentionally preserve a strong board and spend resources to
maintain a winning streak.

## Loss Streaks

A player may intentionally accept losses in exchange for economic benefits
and potentially improved access to carousel resources.

## Important RAG Rule

Economy thresholds, streak rewards, XP costs, shop odds, and other numerical
rules should be stored separately by set/patch because Riot may change them.
