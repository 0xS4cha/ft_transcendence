# Reroll Mechanics

## Shop Refresh

Rerolling refreshes the displayed units in the shop at a cost of gold. Each reroll allows access to new possible units.

## Reroll Cost

Reroll costs are fixed per refresh and depend on player level:
- Higher player levels have higher reroll costs
- Costs scale from 1 gold at level 1 to 5 gold at level 9

## Gold Efficiency

Efficient rerolling balances:
- Cost of rerolls against benefit gained
- Number of rerolls needed to hit target units
- Opportunity cost of gold spent

## Shop Odds

Each player level has specific odds for unit rarities in the shop:
- Lower levels favor cheaper units
- Higher levels access expensive units
- Odds create probabilities, not guarantees

## Hyperroll Strategy

Hyperrolling involves spending most gold rerolling aggressively at a specific level to find core units.

## Levels and Reroll Strategy

Different player levels suit different reroll strategies:
- Level 5 hyperroll targets 2-cost units
- Level 6 hyperroll targets 3-cost units
- Level 7-8 transitions to economy

## Variance in Rerolling

Rerolling involves significant variance:
- Sometimes hitting instantly
- Sometimes requiring many refreshes
- Affecting overall game trajectory significantly

## When to Stop Rerolling

Successful players know when to stop rerolling and transition to economy or leveling based on health, board strength, and remaining resources.
