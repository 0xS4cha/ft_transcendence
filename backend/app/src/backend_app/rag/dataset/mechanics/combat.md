# TFT Combat

## Overview

Teamfight Tactics is an auto-battler in which players assemble a team of units,
equip them with items, position them on a board, and fight other players'
teams automatically.

## Combat Flow

1. Units begin combat according to their starting positions.
2. Units acquire targets according to targeting rules.
3. Units perform basic attacks and use abilities.
4. Traits, items, augments, and abilities modify combat.
5. Combat ends when one side has no surviving units.

## Key Concepts

### Attack Damage
Attack Damage affects the damage dealt by basic attacks and abilities that
scale from Attack Damage.

### Ability Power
Ability Power generally increases the effectiveness of magic-damage abilities
and other abilities that explicitly scale with AP.

### Attack Speed
Attack Speed determines how frequently a unit performs basic attacks.

### Mana
Mana is used by units to cast their abilities. Units generally gain mana
through attacking and taking damage, with exact behavior depending on the
unit and current game rules.
