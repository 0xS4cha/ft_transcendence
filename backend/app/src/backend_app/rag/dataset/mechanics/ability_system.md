# TFT Ability System

## Unit Abilities

Each unit has unique abilities that trigger during combat:
- Passive abilities always active
- Active abilities triggered by conditions (time, health, mana)
- Combinations of passive and active

## Ability Power (AP)

Ability Power increases ability damage and effects:
- Items and traits grant AP
- Higher AP multiplies ability impact
- Key stat for ability-focused units

## Mana and Ability Execution

Units generate mana during combat:
- Starting mana determines first ability timing
- Maximum mana determines ability costs
- Higher attack speed increases mana generation speed

## Ability Timing

Critical ability timing includes:
- First ability cast (determines early teamfight outcome)
- Subsequent abilities (determine extended teamfight)
- Ability cascades (multiple abilities trigger in sequence)

## Crowd Control Abilities

Many units provide crowd control:
- Stuns lock enemies in place
- Slows reduce speed
- Knockbacks displace enemies
- Silences prevent abilities

## Damage Abilities

Some units provide primary damage through:
- Single-target damage
- Area-of-effect damage
- True damage (ignores resistances)

## Utility Abilities

Support units provide utility through:
- Healing and shielding
- Buffs to allies
- Debuffs to enemies
- Mana effects

## Ability Interactions

Understanding how abilities interact with positioning and items helps optimize teamfight outcomes through ability timing.
