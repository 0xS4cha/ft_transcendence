# Star Level Mechanics

## Unit Rarity and Star Levels

Units have rarity tiers (1-cost through 5-cost) and star levels indicating upgrade status.

## Star Level Progressions

Each unit can be upgraded through star levels:
- 1-Star: Base unit from shop
- 2-Star: Combine three 1-star copies
- 3-Star: Combine three 2-star copies (9 total originals)

## Statistical Improvements

Each star level increase significantly boosts:
- Health
- Attack damage
- Ability power
- Survivability and damage output

## Three-Star Challenge

Achieving three-star units is extremely difficult and requires:
- High dedication to rolling for specific unit
- Accepting weak board state during collection
- Significant gold commitment

## Prioritization Strategy

Players typically three-star:
- 1-cost units (most achievable)
- Key carry units if items align
- Rarely attempt multiple three-stars simultaneously

## Diminishing Returns

Early-game two-stars provide significant power while three-stars usually occur only in extended games.

## Strategic Three-Star Wins

Successful three-star pivots require careful economic management and correct timing to avoid premature elimination.

## Star Level vs. Count Variability

RNG determines star level probability, requiring players to accept variance while managing expectations.
