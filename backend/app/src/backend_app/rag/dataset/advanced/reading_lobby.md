# Reading the Lobby

## Identifying Opponents' Directions

Observing opponent boards reveals their strategies:
- Trait combinations they're building
- Unit cost focus (low-cost, mid-cost, high-cost)
- Item distributions suggesting theme

## Predicting Pivots

Understanding opponent synergies helps predict when they might pivot or what they'll transition to.

## Duplicate Unit Analysis

If multiple opponents are building the same unit or trait, that unit becomes contested and harder to secure.

## Item Economy Reading

Observing opponent items reveals their item luck and helps identify what items might be available in creep rounds.

## Economic Positioning

Tracking opponent gold amounts helps understand:
- Who can afford leveling or rolling
- Who might stabilize late
- Who's in danger of elimination

## Threat Assessment

Determining which opponents pose immediate threats and which are weak helps guide positioning choices.

## Uncontested Direction Identification

Sometimes most lobbies ignore a particular comp, creating an opportunity to execute it uncontested.

## Lobby Meta

Understanding which comps and directions your specific lobby is playing helps inform your own strategy.

## Adjustment Based on Reads

Quality lobby reading should directly inform:
- Whether to pivot
- Whether to level or roll
- How to position
- Which direction to pursue
