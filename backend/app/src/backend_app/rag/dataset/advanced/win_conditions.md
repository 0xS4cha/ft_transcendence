# Win Conditions

## What Is a Win Condition?

A win condition is your path to victory:
- Which units or traits will carry your team
- How you'll leverage them in late-game
- What outcomes you need to achieve

## Identifying Your Win Condition

As a game develops, successful players identify:
- Their primary carry
- Supporting units and traits
- Items needed for power spikes

## Multiple Win Condition Paths

Many comps have several possible win conditions:
- Path A: Three-star carry
- Path B: Natural items + two-star carry
- Path C: Leveling to access 4 and 5-cost units

## Probabilistic Win Assessment

Assessing win probability based on:
- Remaining health and gold
- Board strength vs. opponents
- Items and units available

## Late-Game Win Scenarios

Understanding exact board matchups needed helps calculate win outs:
- Who do you need to face?
- What positions win those matchups?
- What health do you need to survive?

## Pivot-Induced Win Condition Changes

Pivoting changes your win condition:
- New carry selection
- New trait focus
- New economic timeline

## Economic Win Conditions

Sometimes winning requires:
- Reaching level 9 for specific units
- Rolling at specific power spikes
- Securing hyperroll targets

## Accept Elimination

Sometimes win conditions are mathematically impossible based on current state, requiring graceful acceptance.
