# Advanced Decision Making

## Decision Trees

Skilled players evaluate decisions using mental models of outcomes:
- What are possible results?
- What's my optimal outcome?
- What's my worst case?

## Risk Assessment

Every decision involves risk:
- Rolling all gold risks weak position
- Saving gold risks staying behind
- Pivoting risks whiffing target units

## Expected Value Calculation

Professional players calculate expected value of decisions:
- Will this roll likely improve my board?
- Is this levelup worth the economy cost?
- Should I risk this pivot?

## Scenario Planning

Looking ahead multiple rounds helps plan:
- When you'll level next
- What gold you'll have
- What units might be available

## Adaptation vs. Stubbornness

Successful adaptation requires knowing when to:
- Stick with original plan (commitment)
- Pivot to new direction (flexibility)
- Accept losses (loss-streaking)

## Emotional Control

Top players maintain calm decision-making despite:
- Unfortunate RNG outcomes
- Pressure from opponents
- Mounting losses

## Information Integration

Combining scouting information with game state to make informed decisions about current strategy viability.

## Speed of Thought

Tournament players must make decisions quickly while maintaining quality, balancing thoroughness with time constraints.
