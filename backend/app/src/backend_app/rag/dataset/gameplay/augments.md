# TFT Augments

## What Are Augments?

Augments are power-ups that players choose at certain points during a game. They provide various bonuses ranging from small stat increases to game-changing mechanics.

## Augment Offering System

At specific intervals (usually stages 2, 3, and 4), players are offered a choice of three augments and must select one.

## Augment Tiers

Augments are classified by tier:
- Silver Augments: Common, modest bonuses
- Gold Augments: Uncommon, significant bonuses
- Prismatic Augments: Rare, powerful effects

## Augment Types

Common augment categories include:
- Gold gain augments (accelerate economy)
- Unit-specific buffs (boost particular champions)
- Trait-based enhancements (strengthen your synergies)
- Experience augments (accelerate leveling)
- Combat bonuses (improve battle outcomes)

## Strategic Augment Selection

Choosing the right augment at the right time is crucial:
- Early augments should support your chosen direction
- Mid-game augments strengthen your team
- Late-game augments provide finishing power

## Augment Pivoting

Some augments force composition pivots, making them high-risk but potentially high-reward choices that restructure your entire team.

## Regional and Set Variations

Different TFT sets feature completely different augment pools, requiring players to learn new options each season.
