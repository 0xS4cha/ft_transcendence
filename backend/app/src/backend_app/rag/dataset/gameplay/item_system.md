# TFT Item System

## Item Components

Items in TFT are crafted from components found during combat:
- Basic components: Sword, Chain Mail, Negatron Cloak, etc.
- Completed items: Combine two basic components
- Emblems and Artifacts: Special items with unique effects

## Item Placement

Items are equipped on units to enhance their abilities:
- Damage items increase attack damage or ability power
- Defensive items provide armor or health
- Utility items grant special effects like crowd control

## Spatulas

Spatula is a special component that can create:
- Trait emblems (grant a unit an additional trait)
- Unique items specific to certain units

## Item Optimization

Successful item management involves:
- Prioritizing items for your main carry unit
- Distributing defensive items effectively
- Adapting item choices to the game situation

## Item Pivoting

As compositions change, items can be moved from one unit to another using bench slots, allowing flexible adaptation.

## Component vs. Completed Items

Early game prioritizes collecting components while mid to late game focuses on completing powerful items for key units.

## Item Precedence in Composition Building

Item availability often influences composition choices, with players building around available item components.
