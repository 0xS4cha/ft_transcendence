# Gold Generation and Management

## Gold Sources

Players earn gold through:
- Battle outcomes (2 gold per win, 1 per loss)
- Interest on saved gold (1 gold per 10 saved, max 5)
- Creep rounds (guaranteed gold)
- Augments and special events

## Interest System

Interest rewards gold savings:
- 10 gold = 1 interest
- 20 gold = 2 interest
- Up to 50 gold = 5 interest

## Economy Types

Different economic approaches exist:
- Fast economy: Spend aggressively
- Slow economy: Save and accumulate
- Win-streaking economy: Maintain win streak
- Loss-streak economy: Accept losses to save

## Economic Breakpoints

Key gold thresholds determine decision points:
- 40 gold: Level breakpoint
- 50 gold: Maximum interest
- 10+ gold: Reserve for rolls

## Spending Priorities

Players balance:
- Unit purchases
- Experience purchases (leveling)
- Shop refreshes (rerolls)
- Economic reserves

## Early vs. Late Economy

Early game economics focus on survival while late game prioritizes maximizing gold efficiency through interest and leveling.

## Economic Pivots

Changing strategies often requires economic adjustment:
- Rerolling requires liquid gold
- Leveling requires spending on experience
- Stabilizing requires unit purchases

## Gold Math

Calculating future gold helps predict leveling capacity and roll availability several rounds in advance.
