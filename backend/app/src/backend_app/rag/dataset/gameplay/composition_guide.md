# Building Successful Compositions

## Composition Core Elements

Successful compositions require:
- Primary win condition (carry unit)
- Supporting units providing synergies
- Items enabling primary units
- Traits creating stat bonuses

## Carry Unit Selection

Choosing your carry involves:
- Identifying early powerful units
- Pivoting to available 4 and 5-cost units
- Building items that enable chosen carry
- Recognizing when carries need replacement

## Support Unit Roles

Supporting units contribute:
- Trait activation and synergies
- Crowd control and utility
- Frontline tanking
- Protection of primary carry

## Trait Synergy Building

Creating trait synergies requires:
- Selecting units with beneficial traits
- Reaching activation thresholds
- Balancing multiple trait benefits
- Maximizing stat bonuses

## Flex Slots

Most compositions have 2-3 flex slots for:
- Countering specific opponents
- Utilizing available items
- Pivoting directions
- Adapting to available units

## Bench Depth

Maintaining bench depth provides:
- Options for pivoting
- Insurance against item pivots
- Flexibility for direction changes
- Bench space for unit management

## Composition Archetypes

Common comp types include:
- Fast 8 or 9 level comps
- Hyperroll 5-focused comps
- Economy-focused comps
- Star-level focused comps

## Transition Mapping

Planning unit transitions helps:
- Identify which units are temporary
- Plan progression path
- Minimize wasted gold
- Execute smooth pivots
