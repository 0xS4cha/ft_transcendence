# RNG Management

## Understanding Variance

TFT contains inherent randomness that skilled players learn to manage:
- Unit shop generation is random
- Item component generation is random
- Augment offerings are random
- Combat outcomes have variance

## Acceptance vs. Adaptation

Top players learn to accept poor RNG while focusing on optimal play within constraints.

## Decision Quality vs. Outcomes

Sometimes optimal decisions lead to poor outcomes due to RNG, while poor decisions sometimes luck out. Evaluating decisions by quality not outcome is crucial.

## Probability Understanding

Understanding probabilities helps assess likelihood of hitting needed units:
- Lower level has lower odds for expensive units
- Limited units means lower probability if others have them
- Duplicates among players reduce individual odds

## Preparation for Variance

Successful players prepare by:
- Maintaining multiple viable paths
- Keeping flexible compositions
- Avoiding all-in strategies when possible

## RNG Variance Over Time

Short-term variance can be extreme while long-term results trend toward skill expression.

## Acceptance of Elimination

Sometimes RNG is so poor that optimal elimination is the outcome regardless of play quality.

## Leverage Points for Control

While RNG is inherent, players control:
- Positioning
- Unit selection
- Item allocation
- Economic decisions
- Leveling timing

## Mental Framework

Healthy players maintain perspective that they can only control their decision quality, not outcomes.
