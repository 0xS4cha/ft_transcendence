# TFT Core Mechanics

## Round Structure

Each game of TFT consists of multiple rounds where players face opponents in staged combat. Rounds follow a predictable pattern of preparation and combat phases.

## The Shop

The shop displays available units for purchase each round. Players can refresh the shop (reroll) to see different units, costing gold each time.

## Team Composition

Players build teams by purchasing units and combining them with traits and items. The goal is to create synergies that make the team stronger than opponents.

## Combat System

Combat is automatic once the round starts. Players cannot directly control units but can affect their placements and gear to influence outcomes.

## Star Levels

Units can be upgraded to higher star levels by collecting multiple copies:
- 1-star: Base unit
- 2-star: Requires 3 copies
- 3-star: Requires 9 copies (extremely powerful)

## Board Size and Positioning

Players have limited bench space and board positions. Positioning determines which units engage enemies first and affects tactical outcomes.

## Bench Management

The bench holds extra units waiting to be deployed or sold. Effective bench management is crucial for maintaining flexibility in composition changes.
