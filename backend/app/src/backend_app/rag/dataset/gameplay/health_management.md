# Health Management

## Health as a Resource

Health represents your team's durability. You're eliminated when your health reaches zero.

## Damage Calculations

Opponent team strength determines damage dealt:
- Stronger opponents deal more damage
- Weaker opponents deal less damage
- Star level determines base stats

## Stabilization

When your health is low, stabilization means winning rounds to halt health loss and potentially recover.

## Health Thresholds

Understanding critical health points helps with decision-making:
- 50+ health: Safe to experiment
- 30-50 health: Should stabilize
- 1-30 health: In danger, need immediate improvements

## Win Streaking for Health

Maintaining winning rounds recovers health and provides economic advantage simultaneously.

## Loss Streaking Acceptance

Sometimes accepting losses is correct if it sets up late-game power spikes.

## Health vs. Economy Trade-off

Spending resources to stabilize health sometimes costs economy, requiring careful assessment of whether the investment is worthwhile.

## Late Game Health Scenarios

As rounds progress, health becomes more critical and even small placement improvements matter significantly.

## Projection and Planning

Anticipating future health loss helps plan leveling and rolling before crisis situations develop.

## Clutch Stabilization

Competitive players execute clutch stabilization by finding unexpected wins when health is critically low.
