# TFT Trait System

## What Are Traits?

Traits are classifications that units belong to. When you have enough units of a particular trait on your board, you activate synergies that grant bonuses to all units with that trait.

## Trait Thresholds

Each trait has activation thresholds (usually 3, 5, 7, etc.) where:
- 3 units: First activation tier bonus
- 5 units: Second activation tier bonus
- 7+ units: Maximum bonus tier

## Types of Trait Bonuses

Traits grant various bonuses:
- Attack speed increases
- Ability power increases
- Health regeneration
- Armor and magic resistance
- Special effects (stuns, shields, etc.)

## Trait Combinations

Successful teams often combine multiple traits. For example, a team might have a primary trait (main synergy) and secondary traits (supporting synergies).

## Trait Pivoting

As the game progresses, players may "pivot" their composition by shifting units to activate different traits and adapt to available units.

## Set-Specific Traits

Each TFT set introduces its own unique traits, creating distinct meta compositions and strategic approaches.

## Trait Overlap

Many units belong to multiple traits, allowing flexible composition building and multiple synergy paths.
