# Positioning Guide

## Board Layout

The TFT board consists of 28 positions arranged in a 4x7 grid where units fight enemies across the middle line.

## Front-Line Placement

Front-line units should be placed forward to:
- Engage enemies first
- Absorb damage
- Protect backline units

## Carry Placement

Carries should be placed where they can execute their role:
- Protected from priority targets
- In position to deal damage or utility
- Positioned for favorable matchups

## Defensive Positioning

Against enemies with specific threats:
- Position healers near units taking damage
- Protect vulnerable units behind tanks
- Use positioning to deny ability execution

## Threat Assessment

Understanding enemy composition helps determine:
- Where enemies will engage
- Which of your units are threatened
- Optimal counter-positioning

## Spatial Separation

Spreading units out can protect against area-of-effect abilities while clustering can enhance shared buffs.

## Flank Protection

Protecting flanks prevents enemies from wrapping around your formation and targeting your backline units directly.

## Dynamic Adjustments

Successful players adjust positioning frequently based on opponent team comp, incoming threats, and ability timings.

## Standard vs. Specific Positioning

While certain positioning principles apply broadly, specific comps require tailored positioning strategies.
