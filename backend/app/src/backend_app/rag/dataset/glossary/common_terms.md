# TFT Glossary

## Carry

A unit expected to contribute a large portion of the team's damage or utility.

## Tank

A durable unit intended to absorb damage and/or provide frontline utility.

## Frontline

Units positioned to engage enemies and absorb incoming damage.

## Backline

Units positioned away from the main frontline, often containing ranged
damage dealers.

## 1-Cost

A unit whose shop purchase price is one gold.

## 2-Cost

A unit whose shop purchase price is two gold.

## 3-Cost

A unit whose shop purchase price is three gold.

## 4-Cost

A unit whose shop purchase price is four gold.

## 5-Cost

A unit whose shop purchase price is five gold.

## Reroll

Refreshing the shop by spending gold.

## Slow Roll

Rolling while deliberately maintaining a particular economy threshold.

## Fast 8

A strategy focused on reaching level 8 relatively quickly.

## Fast 9

A strategy focused on reaching level 9 relatively quickly.

## Stabilize

Spend resources to make the current board strong enough to survive.

## Contest

When multiple players are pursuing the same or overlapping units or
composition.

## Pivot

Changing from one intended composition to another.

## BiS

"Best in slot"; the items considered especially effective for a particular
unit in a particular context.

## Slam

Immediately building a completed item rather than holding components for a
potentially ideal combination later.

## Flex

Playing adaptively based on the units, items, augments, and lobby state.
